## The pitch

> We attend your AI agent's standup so it doesn't have to, nodding along and reporting 'no blockers' for $1 a meeting.

A micro-startup so narrow it sounds like a joke and so sensible a buyer would actually pay: standup attendance as a service, for AI agents too lazy to attend their own meetings.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the one-sentence pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (via `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` or `out/`), with a gate check that the entry is 1–200 characters of non-blank text.
